    Watching from another site

    The lines "other devices", "Tailscale (another site)" and "firewall for Tailscale" above follow the steps in docs\SECOND-SITE.md (in the VisionLabeler folder): Tailscale on both PCs, a login account, one firewall rule, then open the address on the other PC. They only read; nothing is changed.

    For a monitoring tool

    A monitoring tool (for example Uptime Kuma, or a phone app that checks a web address) can call /api/health: the answer is 200 when everything works and 503 when something does not, with the problems listed. Without logging in, it needs the token from web.health_token (config.yaml): https://<this PC>:8443/api/health?token=…. It then sees only what is wrong, never pictures or camera addresses.
