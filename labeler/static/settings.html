Cameras

Source: a webcam number (0, 1, …), a video or picture file, or a camera address (rtsp://user:password@192.168.1.50:554/stream1). A saved password shows as •••; leave it to keep it. A camera's name cannot be changed later (zones, alert rules and recorded people use it). Removing a camera keeps its recorded people until their retention ends. Empty "Keep days" and "Detector size" use the general values below.

Keeping data

0 = keep forever. Older people, their pictures and clips are deleted every hour.
0 = keep forever.

Backups

The database is backed up every night while the app runs (stop.bat also makes one). The newest 7 are kept in data\backups; once a week the newest is test-restored. The Health page shows how old the newest is.
Another disk or a USB drive: a backup on the same disk is lost with the disk. The folder must exist.

Privacy and access

Viewer accounts see blurred faces in the live video and pictures; admins see faces.
Nobody (admins too) sees faces in pictures saved from now on. Labeling still works; the Review and Answer key pages cannot show faces.

Power

When nobody is in view, the camera is checked only a few times a second (the live picture too), which saves power. The first person seen brings it back to full speed at once.
