Backups

The database is backed up every night while the app runs (stop.bat also makes one). The newest 7 are kept in data\backups; once a week the newest is test-restored. The Health page shows how old the newest is.
Another disk or a USB drive: a backup on the same disk is lost with the disk. The folder must exist.

Fall alerts

When someone standing is down a moment later and stays down, every open page shows a red pop-up with a sound until OK is clicked (the alert rule "Fall" on the Alerts page). Needs a camera that sees the room from the side, not from the ceiling; turn it off per camera above for cameras that never see the floor. Mark beds and sofas on the Live page (Zones: "Lying is normal here").
Before it counts as a fall. Shorter tells you sooner but also when someone just lies down on the floor for a moment; 10 was measured (README "Fall alerts").
One hand held above the head is a silent call for help: a red pop-up with a sound on every open page until OK, like a fall (the alert rule "Help sign" on the Alerts page).
How long the hand must stay up. Shorter is easier for someone hurt, but a hand raised for other reasons (reaching a shelf, stretching) counts sooner; 3 had no false alarms on the measured videos (README "Help sign").

Home / Away

One line per time the house is empty: days (mon, tue, wed, thu, fri, sat, sun, a range like mon-fri, or daily) and times; 23:00-06:00 runs into the next morning. Empty = only the Home / Away button at the top of every page. A button press holds until the schedule next changes. Alert rules use it with "Home / Away" on the Alerts page.

Scene questions

Yes/no questions about a camera's whole picture ("Is the gate open?"), answered by the vision model when it has nothing else to do. The questions are written on the Alerts page.
How many matching answers in a row before an alert: 2 means one wrong answer never alerts; 1 alerts sooner but also on a single mistake.
An alert when nobody was seen on a kiosk camera for a long time while someone should be in (the built-in rule "No movement": 4 hours, 08:00-22:00, only when the house is set to Home). Change the hours or the time on the Alerts page.

Safety checks

On cameras with "Safety" ticked (security cameras by default): when a quick check sees a possible incident (a person down, fire, a fight...), the vision model looks at the last seconds and an alert comes only if it confirms. Each kind is also a rule on the Alerts page.
Off here: the check does not run at all (no camera time, no vision model time). A rule turned off on the Alerts page still checks but does not alert. Falls: the Fall alerts switch above (on safety cameras the vision model checks them too). Suspicious behaviour is climbing, a restricted area, pacing, and the camera covered or turned.
For example 22-06 (from 22:00 until 06:00). At night (or when Away) pacing and a restricted area alert, and climbing is critical.

Vehicles

Cars, motorbikes, bicycles, buses and lorries on the cameras ticked in the "Vehicles" column above (security cameras by default): listed on the Live page, searchable, counted, usable in alert rules. No number plates are read. The vehicle detector about doubles the detector's work per camera.
A vehicle standing still this long is "parked": one record however long it stays.

Animals

Found by the vehicle detector in the same pass (no extra work) on the cameras ticked in the "Animals" column above (security cameras by default): listed on the Live page, searchable, counted, usable in alert rules. Monkeys and monitor lizards are not something it knows. Animals do not wake idle mode or start recording.

Recording

H.264 videos of each camera, watched on the Recordings page (viewers see faces blurred). Needs FFmpeg (the Health page says whether it is found).
Older ones are deleted every hour.
All recordings together; the oldest are deleted first. About 9-13 GB per camera per day recording a busy street all the time; much less at home recording only while people are in view.
Before a recording is deleted, the part around each alert in it is kept this long (0 = not kept).

Power

When nobody is in view, the camera is checked only a few times a second (the live picture too), which saves power. The first person seen brings it back to full speed at once.
How long nobody must be in view first.

Learning

Retrains the fast classifiers on your cameras' people once a week; a new version is kept only if it scores at least as well on the answer key.

Updates

New versions come from your GitHub repository. Update now backs up the database, adds the new version (only if nothing here was changed by hand), installs new Python packages when needed, lets the new version open a copy of the database (the safe-update test) and restarts; if something goes wrong, the version running now stays. Checked once a day by itself. What's new lists what each version added; the first-run check shows whether everything works after an update.
