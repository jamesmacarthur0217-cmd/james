New alert rule

Get notified on every open page when someone matching these conditions appears. With no conditions, the rule matches anyone (e.g. a camera that should see nobody at night). "fell" is a fall: the built-in rule "Fall" pops up with a sound on every page until OK is clicked; turn it off below to stop fall alerts (deleted, it comes back at the next start). "Home / Away" = only when the switch at the top of every page says Away (or Home); its weekly schedule is on the Settings page. "stayed in area" = someone (or, with "vehicle", a vehicle) inside an area drawn on the Live page at least that long, e.g. at the gate for 2 minutes. "animal" makes it a rule about animals (dogs, cats, birds), with an area, the time of day, Home / Away or how long it stayed.
